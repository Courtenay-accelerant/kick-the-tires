# Known-problem list

**Last verified: 2026-11-20.** This list ages. Before cutting or flagging a car, re-check its entry against the source, and check the specific VIN with the manufacturer or a dealer. Being "on the list" means: check whether this car was repaired or covered. It does not mean the car is automatically bad.

A settlement or warranty extension shows a manufacturer acknowledged an issue for those years. Many extended warranties have now expired by age or mileage, so the issue matters more than the coverage.

## Adding an entry
Only add an issue that has a primary or reputable source (an NHTSA document, a manufacturer bulletin, a court filing or settlement site, or established automotive press). Give the years exactly as the source states them, link the source, and update the "Last verified" date.
